## Literature clarification

The proposal's short citation 'Shu et al., 2024' has no title. A relevant primary-source candidate is [RAH by Yubo Shu and colleagues](https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.09904), available as a 2023 preprint. Its assistant architecture includes learning, action, criticism and reflection around user alignment, unlike this project's fixed command compiler. This supports narrowing the novelty claim; it does not prove it is the exact reference intended by the incomplete citation. The current paper already avoids claiming that every prior system isolates one mechanism.
