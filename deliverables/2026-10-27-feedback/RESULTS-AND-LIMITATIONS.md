## What the professor asked, in one defensible answer

"We can show that an explicit category boost or mute changes the fixed feed, and the offline evaluator can count that change. The E1 canonical rule and Llama results are equal, so that part shows the intervention works, not why an LLM is needed. The separately frozen fresh E2 gives the LLM a stronger parser result than simple rules on its synthetic model-reference bank, but references are AI-generated, conditional controls failed, and the ranking-quality proxy fell. We have not measured real user satisfaction. A manual intent reference and matched downstream comparison would be the next prospective study if you want the original human-intent claim tested."
